# 1. Five Real-World Friction / Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | What Actually Happens | Impact |
|---|------------|----------------------|--------|
| 1 | 🕐 **Unpredictable Waiting Time** | Patients arrive without knowing how many people are ahead of them and may wait 30–120+ minutes. | Frustration, wasted time, overcrowded waiting rooms |
| 2 | 📞 **Manual Appointment & Token Booking** | Patients often call the clinic or physically visit to get a token. Receptionists maintain registers or WhatsApp messages. | Double bookings, missed calls, manual errors |
| 3 | 👥 **Overcrowded Clinics** | Many patients arrive around the same time because they don't know the actual queue status. | Crowding, poor patient experience, pressure on staff |
| 4 | 🔄 **Queue Changes Are Hard to Communicate** | A doctor may get delayed, take an emergency patient, or finish consultations faster or slower than expected. | Patients don't know whether to wait, come later, or leave |
| 5 | 🗂️ **Poor Coordination Between Roles** | Receptionist, doctor, patient, and clinic admin may use separate systems or manual communication. | Information gaps, inefficient workflow, difficulty tracking appointments |

# 2. Persona Matrix

This system has **4 primary personas**: Patient, Receptionist, Doctor, and Clinic Admin.

| Persona | Patient | Receptionist | Doctor | Clinic Admin |
|---------|---------|--------------|--------|--------------|
| **Role** | Books appointment/token and waits for consultation | Manages appointments and queue | Consults patients and controls consultation flow | Manages clinic, doctors, and overall operations |
| **Main Goal** | Get consultation without unnecessary waiting | Keep queue organized | See patients efficiently | Monitor clinic performance |
| **Main Pain Point** | Doesn't know actual waiting time | Manual queue management | Unpredictable patient flow | Lack of operational visibility |
| **Current Behavior** | Calls/visits clinic or waits physically | Uses registers, phone, or WhatsApp | Calls the next patient manually | Uses spreadsheets/manual reports |
| **Key Need** | Live queue position | Simple queue dashboard | "Next Patient" workflow | Analytics & management dashboard |
| **Important Feature** | Live token + ETA | Token/queue management | Patient queue | Reports & user management |
| **Frustration** | "How long do I have to wait?" | "Which patient is next?" | "Who should I call next?" | "How is my clinic performing?" |
| **Success Metric** | Reduced waiting uncertainty | Faster queue handling | Smooth consultations | Better clinic utilization |
